Overview

A bond's price moves opposite to its yield. Two measures describe how far: duration and convexity.

Duration

Duration is a bond's price sensitivity to a change in yield. Higher duration means bigger swings.

PRICE–YIELD CURVE

When yields rise, the price slides down the curve, and long-duration bonds fall furthest.

Convexity

Convexity is the curve's bend. It makes price gains a little bigger than price losses.

EXERCISE 2 OF 4Yields rise 1%. A bond with duration 7…
AGains value
BLoses about 7%
Correct. Price falls by about duration × the change in yield.
Price change
%ΔP ≈ −Duration × Δy−7 × 1% ≈ −0.0%
