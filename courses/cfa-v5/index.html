Dividend Discount Models
Valuing a stock by its dividends

A share is worth the present value of its future dividends, discounted at the required return.

If dividends grow at a constant rate forever, the sum collapses to one line:

GORDON GROWTH MODEL
V₀ = D₁ ÷ (r − g)
D₁$2.00r9%g4%→V₀$40.00

Here $2.00 ÷ (9% − 4%) gives a value of $40.00 per share.

The model only works when the required return is above the growth rate.
